Flagship

Prevost H3-45

Luxury touring motorcoach

The H3-45 is what touring artists and professional sports teams ride. It sits higher than a standard coach, so passengers see over the guardrail instead of through it, and it carries the largest underfloor luggage volume in its class — which matters when a team travels with equipment.

Mid-size

Temsa TS 30

31-foot mid-size coach

A full coach experience in a shorter body. The TS 30 keeps the underfloor luggage bays, the reclining seats, and the onboard restroom, but its 27-foot turning radius fits campus loops, historic districts, and driveways a 45-footer cannot enter.

Maintenance

We service what we drive

Our Slippery Rock facility handles our own fleet and supports other operators traveling through western Pennsylvania.

Prevost authorized service center

Our Slippery Rock shop is authorized by Prevost and is the go-to facility for Millennium Luxury Coaches and for Florida Coaches, one of America’s top entertainer coach operators. Preventive maintenance runs on a set schedule, and AC service, tire work, and windshield repair are handled on site rather than farmed out.

Coach cleaning

Every coach is detailed before it leaves the yard. A 12-hour trip should start on a clean coach.

Replacement coaches

If a coach has trouble on the road, we send another one. A mechanical problem does not end your trip.

Support for other operators

Fueling, towing, lavatory dump, tire repair, and emergency service for visiting motorcoaches. Call dispatch for availability.

Want a coach for your dates?

Availability moves fastest in spring and fall. Send your trip and we will confirm what we have.
