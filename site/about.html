About us

Over 54 years of moving groups out of Slippery Rock, Pennsylvania.

Campbell Bus Lines has been family owned and operated since the 1970s. Todd Campbell runs it today, from the same town it started in, and serves on the board of directors of the United Motorcoach Association.

Our story

A regional operator that never outgrew its standards

We started as a small Butler County bus company and grew by doing the same thing well for a long time: getting groups where they are going, safely and on schedule. Fifty-four years later, the ownership is still the Campbell family and the office is still on Grove City Road.

What changed is the equipment. We kept reinvesting — Prevost H3-45 luxury touring coaches for full groups, Temsa TS 30 mid-size coaches for smaller ones — which is unusual for an operator our size and the reason so many of our clients rebook without shopping around.

We move college athletic programs, school districts, summer camps, military relocations, corporate groups, and multi-day tours across the country. Many of the groups riding with us this season have been riding with us for a decade or more.
