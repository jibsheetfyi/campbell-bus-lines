What that means

A new fleet is not a bragging point. It is how your trip goes right.

Maximum uptime

A coach that is new is a coach that is available. When you book a date, you get the coach you were quoted, not a substitute pulled off another job.

Minimal downtime

The trip nobody forgets is the one that stops on the shoulder. Our Slippery Rock shop is a Prevost authorized service center, so the coach is serviced to the factory standard by the same team that drives it out the door.

Latest safety systems

Current-model coaches carry current-model electronics. The Temsa TS 30 runs Bendix stability control with ABS and traction control as standard, and our Prevost H3-45 coaches are specified the same way.

Better fuel economy

The H3-45’s Volvo D13 pairs with an Allison FuelSense 2.0 transmission, and the TS 30 runs a right-sized Cummins B6.7. Fewer gallons per mile is a number that shows up in your quote.

Why Campbell

One family, one standard, every trip.

We are not a broker. Every coach that shows up for your group is ours, maintained in our own Slippery Rock shop and driven by a Campbell operator we know by name.

  • Two coach sizes, one standard. A 56-passenger Prevost H3-45 or a 34-passenger Temsa TS 30, so you are not paying for seats your group will not fill.
  • Top DOT safety rating. Federally rated Satisfactory, with $5 million in liability coverage on file.
  • A Prevost authorized service center. Our maintenance team holds Prevost’s own authorization, and it is the shop Millennium Luxury Coaches and Florida Coaches — one of the top entertainer coach operators in America — send their equipment to. We believe there is no better shop in the Northeast.
  • Repeat clients, not one-off bookings. Athletic departments, districts, and camps rebook with us season after season.
  • Real people answering the phone. Dispatch you can reach directly, including while your group is on the road.
