Charter services

Charter motorcoach service, planned by people who dispatch it themselves.

One point of contact from quote to drop-off. No brokers, no substituted equipment, no surprises on the invoice.

How it works

Four steps from inquiry to departure

01

Send the trip

Dates, headcount, pickup, destination, and anything unusual about the itinerary. Email the quote form or call the office.

02

Get a firm quote

We check coach and driver availability against your dates and quote the actual trip, including driver hours and overnight requirements.

03

Confirm and plan

We lock the coach, assign your driver, and work through routing, parking, and timing details with your group leader.

04

Travel

Your driver arrives early, dispatch stays reachable for the whole trip, and the same coach stays with your group start to finish.

Athletic travel

Built around game-day schedules

Campbell is the transportation partner for Grove City College athletics, and we run team travel for programs across western Pennsylvania and Ohio.

  • Equipment and luggage capacity underneath, so gear does not ride in the aisle.
  • Late returns and overnight stays planned inside federal driver-hour rules, not around them.
  • Video and PA onboard for film review or a pre-game walk-through on the way in.
  • Season-long scheduling for programs that would rather book once than trip by trip.

Other group work
