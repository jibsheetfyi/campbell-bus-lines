Employment application

Ten minutes, one page. Have your CDL class and endorsements, your recent employment history, and your medical examiner’s certificate handy.

Applicant
Licensing & qualifications
Do you have a Passenger (P) endorsement?
Do you have an air brake endorsement, with no air brake restriction?
Do you have a School Bus (S) endorsement?
Do you have a current Medical Examiner’s Certificate (DOT physical card)?
Can you provide an official state driving record issued within the last 30 days?

If not, we will ask for one before your application is considered. In Pennsylvania you can request a certified driving record from PennDOT.
