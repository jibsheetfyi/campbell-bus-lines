Drive with us

We are hiring motorcoach operators.

Full-time, part-time, and seasonal work out of Slippery Rock, on the newest motorcoaches in America. CDL holders and candidates willing to train are both welcome.

The job

Professional charter driving, not a route

Campbell operators take athletic teams, school groups, camps, and tour clients across the country. One day may be a local shuttle; the next may be a multi-day trip with a hotel stay covered.

  • Late-model equipment. Prevost H3-45 and Temsa TS 30 coaches, maintained in our own Prevost authorized service center.
  • Flexible schedules. Full-time, part-time, and seasonal work, including options that suit retirees.
  • Paid travel expenses on overnight and multi-day trips.
  • Home base in Slippery Rock, with most trips originating within Butler County.

Company uniform, air-ride driver seat, and a coach that came out of our own shop.

Requirements

What we look for

CDL with passenger endorsement

Class B CDL or higher with a passenger endorsement. Air brake restriction must be cleared. We will discuss training paths with strong candidates who are not yet licensed.

Clean driving record

An MVR that meets federal and insurer standards, plus DOT physical and drug-screen compliance.

Customer presence

You are the face of the company for a school, a team, or a touring group. Professional, patient, and presentable matters as much as skill behind the wheel.

Flexibility

Charter work moves. Weekends, early departures, and overnight trips are part of the schedule.
